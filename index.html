What is SketchDeck.ai?

SketchDeck.ai is a B2B SaaS company that provides AI-powered material counting software, LIFT, to help estimators in structural steel fabrication companies automate their steel takeoffs. Our software extracts data from uploaded blueprints, enabling teams to complete projects faster and more accurately.

How does LIFT work?

LIFT uses AI technology to analyze uploaded blueprints and automatically count the materials needed for a project. It eliminates the need for manual counting, saving time and improving accuracy.

What are the benefits of using SketchDeck.ai?

By using SketchDeck.ai, estimators in structural steel fabrication companies can complete projects in minutes instead of days. Our software improves accuracy, reduces errors, and helps teams manage resources effectively.

Is SketchDeck.ai suitable for small teams?

Yes, SketchDeck.ai is designed to be scalable and can be used by both small and large estimator teams. Our software is flexible and can adapt to the needs of different companies.

How can I get started with SketchDeck.ai?

To get started with SketchDeck.ai, simply contact us through the form below. Our team will be happy to assist you and provide further information.

What can I expect on a demo?

During our demo, we will analyze one of your own project drawings and provide you with a free project in LIFT to see if it’s a good fit for your company.
LIFT automates data extraction from drawings, creating accurate Bills of Materials quickly and effortlessly.
